WiFiChat Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-27 · Developer: esrrhs · Contact: esrrhs@163.com

Welcome to WiFiChat (Android package com.esrrhs.wifichat, the “App”), developed by esrrhs (“we”). Please read this policy before use. By continuing to use the App, you acknowledge this policy. If you disagree, stop using the App.

1. Information we collect and use

WiFiChat is an offline, zero-association nearby communication tool that exchanges encrypted messages and files over local 802.11 Wi-Fi SSID beacons and scans. We do not run any servers, user accounts, analytics, or advertising SDKs, and we do not collect, upload, or sell any personal information, location data, or device identifiers.

Permissions and data processed strictly on your device:

  • Location & Nearby Wi-Fi Devices (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES): Required by Android to scan nearby Wi-Fi SSIDs so the App can receive nearby encrypted beacons. Location coordinates are never queried, stored, or transmitted.
  • Ephemeral Cryptographic Keys: X25519 keypairs and derived session keys exist only in RAM for the current session and are discarded when you rotate keys or exit.
  • Received Files & Preferences: Incoming files are saved locally in the App’s private storage (files/downloads/), and local settings (such as room passphrase or nickname) remain on-device.
  • Foreground Service & Notifications (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to keep the local Wi-Fi scan/broadcast session active and display its status notification.
  • Local Wireless Debugging (Optional): If you choose to activate SoftAP TX via Android Wireless Debugging, pairing and shell commands run strictly over loopback on your device.

2. Storage and protection

All messages are encrypted end-to-end or room-isolated with ChaCha20-Poly1305 AEAD and Argon2id. No cloud backup is enabled (allowBackup="false"). You may clear local data at any time in Android Settings or by uninstalling the App.

3. Sharing and disclosure

We do not collect or share your personal data with third parties. Encrypted Wi-Fi SSID frames are broadcast locally over the air to nearby receivers within radio range.

4. Third parties and open source

The App is open-source software. Google Play may process limited distribution-related data under Google’s own policies.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal data.

6. Changes

We may update this policy on this page with a new effective date. Please review it periodically.
